Harappan civilisation (c. 2600 – 1900 BCE)

Towns were laid out on a grid, with covered drains running beside the main streetsdue and houses built of baked brick of standard size.

The Great Bath at Mohenjo-darodue is the best known public building; its exact use is still debated.

Vedic age (c. 1500 – 600 BCE)

The Rigveda is the oldest textdue; the later Vedic texts show settled farming in the Ganga plains and the growth of varnadue.

The Mughal succession (1707)
